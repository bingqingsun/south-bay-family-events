TERMS & DATA NOTICE

Using this activity guide

Last updated: August 31, 2026. South Bay Family Finds is an independent discovery guide for parents and caregivers. It is not the organizer, ticket seller, venue, or booking agent for listed activities.

Activity information

We organize publicly available information from activity organizers and link to their pages. Dates, times, prices, age guidance, availability, accessibility, weather plans, and cancellation policies can change. Always confirm details and any participation requirements with the organizer before leaving home or buying tickets.

Not affiliated with organizers

Organizer names, activity names, and images remain the property of their respective owners. Listing an activity does not mean that the organizer endorses or is affiliated with South Bay Family Finds.

Images and requests from rights holders

When available, we may display an organizer-provided activity image alongside a direct link to that activity. When an appropriate organizer image is not available, we use an original fallback illustration. If you believe material on this site should be removed or corrected, email hello@southbayfamilyplans.com with the relevant link and a short explanation.

Family decisions and safety

Choose activities based on your family’s needs and the organizer’s current guidance. You are responsible for supervision, transportation, registrations, purchases, and participation decisions.

Current business model

This site currently has no paid listings, affiliate links, or advertising. If that changes, sponsored or commission-bearing content will be clearly labeled and will not affect whether an activity is described as coming from an official organizer source.

Report a correction

See an incorrect, cancelled, or unsuitable listing? Email hello@southbayfamilyplans.com and include the activity link if possible. For privacy practices, see Privacy & analytics.
