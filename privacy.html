PRIVACY

Privacy & analytics

Weekend Plans helps families find South Bay activities. We do not ask for names, email addresses, home addresses, or children’s personal information.

Optional site analytics

If you choose “Allow analytics,” we use Google Analytics to understand aggregated visits, pages viewed, general traffic sources, and how site features are used. Google may process browser, device, and approximate location information as part of this measurement. We do not send a child’s exact age, saved activities, precise location, name, email address, or home address to Analytics.

You can change your choice at any time.
