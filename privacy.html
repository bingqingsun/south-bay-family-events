PRIVACY

Privacy & analytics

Last updated: August 31, 2026. South Bay Family Finds helps parents and caregivers find activities across the South Bay. The site is intended for parents and caregivers; it is not a service for children to create accounts or submit information.

Information we do not collect

We do not require names, email addresses, home addresses, payment information, or children’s personal information to use the site. Please do not send us personal information about a child.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive the email address and message you choose to send. We use them only to reply, investigate a correction, or address your privacy question. We aim to delete completed email requests within 12 months, unless we need to keep them longer to resolve an issue or meet a legal obligation. You may ask us to delete a message sooner.

Optional site analytics

If you choose “Allow analytics,” we use Google Analytics to understand aggregated visits, pages viewed, general traffic sources, and feature use. Google Analytics uses cookies and may process browser, device, and approximate location information. We do not send a child’s exact age, saved activities, precise location, name, email address, or home address to Analytics. Google’s privacy practices apply to its processing.

You can change your choice at any time. If you choose “Use only essential features,” analytics does not load.

Saved activities and essential storage

Saved activities and your analytics choice are stored in your browser on that device. They are not sent to South Bay Family Finds and may be cleared in your browser settings.

Links to organizers

Activity detail links lead to independent organizer websites. Their privacy practices and terms apply once you leave this site.

Questions or updates

For a privacy question or to report an issue, email hello@southbayfamilyplans.com. We will update this page before making a material change to these practices.
