ABOUT & CONTACT

Family time, well spent.

South Bay Family Finds is an independent guide for parents and caregivers—making it easier to find local activities worth doing together.

Official links first

We prioritize direct pages from organizers, venues, libraries, museums, public agencies, and their authorized ticketing partners. A search result can help us find an official page, but it is never the source on its own.

Curated, not exhaustive

We focus on activities that appear relevant to local families and include enough useful detail to help you decide whether to explore further. Every card links back to the organizer for the full picture.

Kept fresh, then double-checked

We refresh participating official sources on weekdays. Plans can still change, so please confirm time, price, availability, accessibility, and registration requirements before you go.

Images with context

When available, we use an organizer-provided activity image to identify the linked event. Otherwise, we use an original illustration. An image never implies a partnership or endorsement.

Contact us

Tell us about an incorrect, cancelled, unsuitable, or missing activity—or ask a privacy or image-use question. Please include the relevant activity link and do not send personal information about a child.
