Plans for South Bay families

South Bay Family Plans is an independent activity guide for parents and caregivers. We help families discover things worth doing across the South Bay, from toddler storytimes to teen workshops, performances, sports, and museum exhibits.

How we choose activities

We prioritize activities with a direct link to an organizer, venue, or public agency’s official page or calendar. We use official calendars, RSS or ICS feeds, and organizer event pages as primary inputs. A search result may help us find an official page, but it is not treated as the activity source on its own.

We do not aim to list every activity in the region. We focus on activities that appear relevant to local families and for which we can provide enough useful detail to help you decide whether to explore further.

What “official source” means

An official source is a page published by the organizer, venue, public agency, library, school district, museum, park district, or ticketing partner authorized by the organizer. Each card links back to that source so you can confirm the latest details.

Updates and accuracy

We refresh participating official sources on weekdays. Activity details can change after a refresh, so please confirm time, location, age guidance, price, availability, accessibility, and registration requirements on the organizer’s page before you go.

Images

We use organizer-provided activity images when available. When no suitable image is available, we use an original fallback illustration. Image use is for identifying the linked activity, not to suggest an organizer partnership or endorsement.

Contact, corrections, and removal requests

To report an incorrect, cancelled, unsuitable, or missing activity—or to ask a privacy or image-use question—email hello@southbayfamilyplans.com. Please include the relevant activity or page link when possible. Do not send personal information about a child.
