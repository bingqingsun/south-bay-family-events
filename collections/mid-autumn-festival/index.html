WHY IT MATTERS

About the Mid-Autumn Festival

The Mid-Autumn Festival, also called the Moon Festival, is celebrated across Chinese, Vietnamese, and other Asian communities. It is a time for families to gather under the full moon, share mooncakes, light lanterns, and celebrate reunion, gratitude, and the harvest.

In Vietnamese communities, Tết Trung Thu often puts children at the center, with lantern parades, lion dances, storytelling, and performances.
